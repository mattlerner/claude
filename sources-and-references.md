## Data Quality and Limitations

### High-Quality Evidence
- Multiple RCTs published in peer-reviewed journals
- GiveWell-reviewed organizations with independent analysis
- CGIAR aggregate impact studies with extensive data

### Moderate-Quality Evidence
- Organization self-reported M&E data
- Single RCTs without replication
- Academic studies in reputable journals but limited sample sizes

### Low-Quality Evidence or Data Gaps
- Cost-per-farmer data often not publicly reported
- Long-term sustainability data (>3 years) rare
- Geographic generalizability unclear
- Selection effects not always controlled

### Critical Missing Data
1. **myAgro:** Exact cost per farmer not found in public sources
2. **Precision Development:** Why GiveWell declined 2023 implementation funding
3. **One Acre Fund:** Program-by-program and geography-by-geography cost-effectiveness breakdown
4. **Extension services:** Implementer-specific cost data for community-based models

## Calculation Methodology

### Cost to Double Income Formula

```
Cost to Double Income = (Cost per Beneficiary) × (100% / Income Increase %)
```

**Example:** One Acre Fund
- Cost: $80 per farmer
- Income increase: 35%
- Cost to double: $80 × (100/35) = $80 × 2.857 = $228.57

**Assumes:** Linear scaling of effects (which may not hold in practice)

### Baseline Income Calculation

```
Baseline Income = Income Increase ($) / Income Increase (%)
```

**Example:** myAgro 2020
- Income increase: $178
- Income increase: 26%
- Baseline: $178 / 0.26 = $684.62

**Note:** This is a back-calculation that assumes the percentage is accurate

### Limitations of These Calculations

1. **Non-linear effects:** Marginal returns may diminish
2. **Heterogeneity:** Different farmers have different baseline incomes and responses
3. **Timeframes:** One-year effects vs. sustained multi-year effects
4. **Measurement:** Income vs. consumption vs. revenue vs. profit
5. **Attribution:** Selection bias (most motivated farmers participate)
6. **Yield vs. Income:** Yield increases don't translate directly to income due to costs

## Corrections to Initial Analysis

### Error 1: Precision Development BCR Interpretation
**Initial claim:** "$1 generates $12-19 in profit" applied directly to $1.80 cost
**Issue:** Unclear whether this is annual, cumulative, or NPV
**Correction:** Marked as requiring clarification; calculation uncertain

### Error 2: Malawi Subsidy Cost Calculation
**Initial claim:** Calculated cost-to-double ratios for fertilizer subsidies
**Issue:** Missing data on cost per kg of subsidy provided
**Correction:** Marked calculations as incomplete; relied on research consensus of poor cost-effectiveness

### Error 3: Baseline Income Assumptions
**Initial approach:** Back-calculated baseline from percentage gains
**Issue:** Assumes reported percentages are accurate and representative
**Correction:** Added note about heterogeneity and that these are estimates based on reported data

### Error 4: Linear Scaling Assumption
**Initial approach:** Assumed doubling income costs 2x more than achieving 50% increase
**Issue:** Marginal returns likely diminish; non-linear effects
**Correction:** Added explicit note about this limitation in all calculations

## Confidence Levels by Data Point

**High Confidence (Multiple sources, RCT evidence, GiveWell-reviewed):**
- One Acre Fund: Cost ~$80, income gain ~40-45%, yield doubling
- Precision Development: Cost ~$1.80/year, positive RCT evidence
- CGIAR: BCR of 6-10:1 in agriculture R&D
- Fertilizer subsidies: Poor cost-effectiveness (research consensus)

**Moderate Confidence (Self-reported with some external validation):**
- myAgro: Income gains of 26-100% (wide range suggests heterogeneity)
- One Acre Fund: Net cost after repayment ~$20-37
- Extension services: Generally positive but cost data sparse

**Low Confidence (Limited sources, calculations based on incomplete data):**
- myAgro: Exact cost per farmer
- Precision Development: Why GiveWell declined 2023 funding
- Extension services: Specific implementer costs
- Exact cost-to-double ratios (due to linear scaling assumption)

**Unknown (Critical data gaps):**
- myAgro cost structure
- One Acre Fund program-by-program cost-effectiveness
- Long-term (>5 year) income sustainability for most programs
- Geographic variation in cost-effectiveness
